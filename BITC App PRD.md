# **Product Requirements Document: Brunch in t' City App**

## **1\. Overview**

The Brunch in t' City App is an all-in-one digital platform designed to be a unified ecosystem for young professionals and creatives. It aims to transition the existing Brunch in t' City events company into a comprehensive app-based hub that seamlessly integrates lifestyle, community, recruitment, mentorship, and creative management tools.

This document outlines the requirements for the initial Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and subsequent mobile app launch, as detailed in the provided pitch decks and mockups.

## **2\. Problem Statement**

The current digital landscape for young professionals and creatives is fragmented. Key services are scattered across multiple platforms: Eventbrite for ticketing, LinkedIn/Indeed for jobs, Instagram for entertainment, and Fiverr/Upwork for freelance work. This disconnected ecosystem leads to:

* Creatives being underpaid and lacking career support.  
* Limited access to essential tools and resources.  
* A lack of a unified hub for networking, creative opportunities, and career growth.

## **3\. Goals & Objectives**

The primary goal is to solve the problem of fragmented platforms by creating a singular, comprehensive hub.

**Business Goals:**

* Redefine how young professionals and creatives connect, create, and thrive.  
* Achieve a Year 1 revenue target of £120k.  
* Reach a Year 3 revenue projection of £1M+.  
* Grow the user base to 50,000+ users within three years.  
* Scale the platform to new territories, including Lagos, Ghana, and South Africa.

**Product Goals:**

* Deliver a unified digital experience that is intuitive and user-friendly.  
* Empower underrepresented creatives with tools and resources.  
* Foster digital skill participation and promote entrepreneurship.  
* Develop a modular tech architecture for global growth.

## **4\. Target Audience**

The target audience is multi-faceted, encompassing both individuals and businesses:

* Freelance creatives (e.g., designers, photographers, videographers).  
* Creative graduates entering the workforce.  
* Small businesses and companies needing creative services.  
* Educational institutions seeking to connect students with opportunities.  
* HR outsourcing teams.

## **5\. Key Features & Functionality**

### **Core Platform Modules**

* **Onboarding & User Profiles:**  
  * Multi-role signup (user, creative, business).  
  * Social login options (Google, LinkedIn).  
  * Create and edit professional creative profiles and portfolios.  
* **Events Hub:**  
  * Discover nearby and upcoming events.  
  * Purchase event tickets directly within the app.  
  * Utilize QR code check-in for seamless entry.  
  * Access networking opportunities at events.  
* **Community Networking:**  
  * A centralized community feed for posting and interacting.  
  * Group chat and direct messaging capabilities.  
  * User discovery to connect with other professionals.  
* **Recruitment & Job Board:**  
  * A dedicated job board with search and filter functionality.  
  * AI-driven job matching to connect creatives with relevant opportunities.  
  * Employer dashboards for posting and managing job applications.  
* **Mentorship Marketplace:**  
  * A directory of experienced mentors with bookable packages.  
  * In-app video call functionality for mentorship sessions.

### **Skills Vault Module (AI-Powered)**

* **AI Tools:**  
  * **AI Portfolio Review:** Provides automated feedback and optimization suggestions for a user's creative portfolio.  
  * **AI Brief Interpreter:** Interprets project briefs to help creatives understand client requirements.  
  * **Dynamic Rate Calculator:** Recommends fair freelance rates based on market data and project details.  
  * **Smart Contract Templates:** Generates and provides secure, legally sound contract templates for client work.  
  * **AI Career Path Generator:** Recommends personalized career paths and learning objectives.  
* **Learning Hub:**  
  * Access a library of tailored courses categorized by skill (e.g., Graphic Design, Photography, Writing, Illustration).  
  * Track progress through courses and view completion percentages.  
* **Creative Management:**  
  * A project dashboard for managing multiple projects.  
  * Track project progress with visual indicators (e.g., a progress bar).  
  * Upload and showcase creative work in a portfolio builder.  
* **Marketplace & Digital Store:**  
  * A dedicated space for creatives to sell their services and digital products (e.g., e-books, templates, photos).  
* **Wellbeing Hub:**  
  * Offers resources and programs focused on mental health and wellness (e.g., stress management, sleep workshops).

## **6\. Development & Release Roadmap**

The development follows a phased approach:

* MVP Build  
* Beta Testing  
* Public Launch of the core web platform.  
* Mobile App Launch (iOS and Android).

Subsequent phases will focus on national and global scaling, including partnerships with universities and brand collaborations.

## **7\. Success Metrics**

* **Revenue:** Achieving the set revenue targets (£120k in Year 1, £1M+ in Year 3).  
* **User Growth:** Reaching 50,000+ users within the first three years.  
* **Engagement:** High user retention, active participation in the community feed, and a high number of courses completed and projects tracked.  
* **Partnerships:** Successful university partnerships and brand collaborations.  
* **Market Impact:** Positive social and economic impact on underrepresented creative communities.
